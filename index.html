Questions

Before you ask.

I'm not a tech person. Will I understand?

Yes. NoteAI is made for normal people, not tech insiders. If you can use WhatsApp, you can follow along.

Is it in Malayalam or English?

Explanations are in everyday Malayalam. Tool names and prompts stay in English, so you can copy and use them directly.

Do I need paid apps?

You can start with free tools. When something needs a paid plan, you'll be told clearly, along with what it can't do.

How do I join a session or invite NoteAI to my college?

Current sessions, availability and details are on the booking page. For talks and workshops, book a slot there or message on Instagram.

Is NoteAI hiring?

Openings are announced on Instagram first. Follow @ansar_noteai to see them when they go up.
